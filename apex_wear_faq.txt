APEX WEAR - CUSTOMER KNOWLEDGE BASE & FAQ
==================================================

1. ABOUT APEX WEAR
APEX WEAR is a premier modern streetwear and athletic apparel brand based in Toronto, Canada. We specialize in high-durability performance fabrics, oversized hoodies, seam-sealed jackets, and technical gym wear. All garments are ethically manufactured using 100% organic cotton and recycled polyester blends.

2. SHIPPING & DELIVERY
- Standard Domestic Shipping (Canada & USA): 3-5 business days via FedEx/PostNL. Standard shipping is FREE on all orders over $75 USD.
- Express Shipping: 1-2 business days ($15 USD flat rate).
- International Shipping: 7-12 business days. Customs duties and taxes are calculated at checkout.
- Order Tracking: Once dispatched, a tracking number is automatically emailed to the customer within 24 hours.

3. RETURNS & EXCHANGES POLICY
- Return Window: Customers can return unworn, unwashed items in their original packaging with tags attached within 30 days of delivery.
- Return Fee: Domestic returns are free. International return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer.
- Non-Returnable Items: Final Sale items, gift cards, and custom teamwear collabs are non-refundable.
- Processing Time: Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after our warehouse receives and inspects the return.

4. SIZING & CARE INSTRUCTIONS
- Sizing: All APEX WEAR tops feature an athletic/oversized cut. If you prefer a snug fit, we recommend sizing down.
- Washing Instructions: Machine wash cold (30°C / 86°F) on a gentle cycle, inside out. 
- Drying Instructions: Do NOT tumble dry. Air dry flat to prevent shrinkage and protect printed vinyl graphics. Do not iron directly over rubberized brand logos.

5. CONTACT & SUPPORT HOURS
- Customer Care Email: support@apexwear.com
- Live Support Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST. Saturday: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM EST. Sunday: Closed.
- Response Time: Our support team responds to all inquiries within 2-4 business hours.
