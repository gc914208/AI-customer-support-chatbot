==================================================
APEX WEAR - WARRANTY, DISCOUNTS & CUSTOM ORDERS
==================================================

1. PRODUCT WARRANTY
- APEX WEAR offers a 90-day Limited Manufacturer Warranty on all outerwear and technical garments.
- Covered Defects: Broken zippers, seam splits, popping stitching, and defective drawstrings.
- Uncovered Items: Normal wear and tear, accidental tears, damage caused by hot tumble dryers, or bleach damage.
- Warranty Claim Process: Email support@apexwear.com with your order number and 2 high-resolution photos of the damaged area.

2. PROMO CODES & DISCOUNTS
- Welcome Offer: New customers get 15% off their first order with code "APEX15".
- Student Discount: Verified students receive a 10% discount through StudentBeans.
- Promo Rules: Only one discount code can be applied per order. Codes cannot be combined with clearance sale items.

3. CUSTOM TEAMWEAR & COLLABS
- Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ): Custom team orders require a minimum of 20 units.
- Lead Time: Custom graphic design and printing take 3-4 weeks from final artwork approval.
- Artwork Requirements: Vector graphics (.SVG or .AI) are required for all custom screen prints.
